Project overview

MVP demo verified live Oct 1. Polish pass in progress.

A text-first POS for single-store dry cleaners and laundromats

Built for the owner-operator who does not want an enterprise system. Customers get updates by text, and the shop runs from a simple counter screen. Full click-through verified Oct 1, 8:42 AM: intake, QR receipt, customer tracking page, pickup.

Key research findings

Cost to serve

Gap

Where competitors are thin

Flat pricing with SMS included, no customer app download, and photo intake.

Incumbents push a branded customer app that people have to download. Hangr is text only: the customer gets a tracking link by text.
